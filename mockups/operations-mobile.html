How it answers the four questions 1. Bottom detail panel → gone. Stat preview lives inside the chip sheet (4-stat grid at top).
2. Leader & Hero → action items in the soldier sheet under "Role".
3. Vehicle has its own chip (blue variant) and its own sheet for squad reassign.
4. Crew chips are blue and show the slot label (TC/GNR/DRV). Tapping opens a crew-specific sheet to swap roles or eject the soldier from the vehicle.
Touch target audit Every chip is 32px tall (visually) but its tap surface extends ~10px around (effectively 44+px with hit-slop). Every row is 48px min. Sheet options are 48px min. No more ghost ✕ buttons — destructive actions live in sheets with proper labeling.
Desktop parity Same UI. Chip tap → popover (not bottom sheet) anchored to the chip. All other behavior identical. Single codepath for the action sheet content.
