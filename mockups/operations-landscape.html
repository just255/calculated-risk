2B — Drawer slides in over dimmed roster.

Drawer — pros:
  • Full-width roster when idle = denser, more units visible at once
  • Drawer is dismissable — clean state when not in use
  • Drawer animation makes the "I just acted on this unit" event feel tactile
Cons:
  • Roster gets occluded by ~45% when drawer is open — can't see what else you're doing
  • One extra tap to dismiss (or tap a different chip)
  • On desktop, the drawer either floats (weird) or pins permanently (becomes Option 1) — splits the implementation
My read: On a landscape phone you have horizontal room to spare and vertical room is the bottleneck. Option 1 (persistent) trades horizontal real estate for not breaking your visual context — you can see which squad has openings while reading the action panel. Option 2 (drawer) trades context for a denser idle roster, but most of the time you'll be actively assigning, so the "idle density" win is mostly theoretical. I'd pick Option 1. It's also the same shape as desktop, which means one implementation.
