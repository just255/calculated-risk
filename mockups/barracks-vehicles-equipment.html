What changes from B:
  • Roster table = soldiers only. No vehicle rows. No "Vehicles" filter chip.
  • Squad pane gets a vehicle slot per squad — empty shows "+ VEHICLE", filled shows vehicle + crew
  • Tap vehicle slot → popover with Motor Pool inventory + actions (Send to Motor Pool, Inspect, etc.)
  • Crew (soldiers in a vehicle) still in the roster — their chip shows "Crew · TC" and tapping moves them between crew slots, infantry, or reserve like before
  • Motor Pool tab gains real purpose: vehicle inventory + repair + (future) loadouts
Conceptual model: People vs. Things. Soldiers are people who join squads. Vehicles are things that squads equip. Crew = a person operating a thing. Sortable columns finally make sense — every roster row is the same shape.
