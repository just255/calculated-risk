Behavior summary:
  • Armory tab = canonical inventory home. Filter chips include category + state (Unassigned/Damaged).
  • Tapping an Armory row could open an item detail drawer (like the vehicle drawer) — repair, destroy, "View on soldier ▸".
  • Soldier dossier gets a new EQUIPMENT tab between PERSONALITY and SERVICE. 2-column slot grid.
  • Tap a slot → picker popover filtered by slot type + soldier MOS. Quality badges and condition visible in the picker.
  • Stats summary at top of EQUIPMENT tab updates as items swap — instant feedback on tradeoffs.
Open question — disabled vs hidden: when an item is incompatible with the soldier's MOS, do we show it greyed-out (current mock) or hide it entirely? Greyed-out educates the player about the restriction; hidden keeps the list tighter. I lean greyed-out for the first few hours, then it's noise.
